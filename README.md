# Studio Launch-Pad

A movie industry analysis built to help a new film studio decide what kinds of
movies are worth making.

## Overview

Our client is entering the movie business for the first time, with no background
in film production. This project uses historical box office, budget, and ratings
data to figure out what actually pays off — and turns that into concrete advice
on what kind of movies they should be making.

## Business Understanding

We're speaking directly to the head of the studio, not a technical audience. They
don't need to know how we did the analysis in detail — they need to know what to
do next.

The questions we're answering:
1. Which genres perform best financially, not just in raw earnings but in return
   on investment?
2. How does production budget relate to profitability — do bigger budgets
   actually pay off?
3. Does critical or audience reception meaningfully affect box office
   performance?

## Data Understanding and Analysis

We pulled together data from several sources to get a full picture of the movie
industry: IMDB (titles, genres, and user ratings), The Numbers (production
budgets and revenue), Box Office Mojo (domestic and international earnings),
Rotten Tomatoes (critic reviews), and TheMovieDB (audience popularity scores).

Each source told us something different — one had the money, one had the
opinions, one had the technical details — so we combined them to see the whole
story, not just a fragment of it.
